ZOA MARKET PRODUCT PHOTOS

The storefront uses the product image paths configured in script.js. Keep the
actual filename and letter casing when editing those paths. JPG and PNG files
are supported, and product photos are displayed without cropping.

Not every catalog item currently has a matching photo. Products without an
image path use a category icon automatically. To add a photo, place it in this
folder and set its relative path on the matching product in script.js.

CHATBOT STOCK ANSWERS

The chatbot only reports a stock status when that product in script.js has an
availability value of "available" or "unavailable". Until the store connects
or supplies a current stock list, it will say that live availability is
unknown and offer to ask the team on WhatsApp. Update these values whenever
stock changes; otherwise the chatbot must not be treated as a live inventory
system.
